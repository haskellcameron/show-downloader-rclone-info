Privacy policy

Show Downloader rclone · Effective September 28, 2026

1. Scope and purpose

Show Downloader rclone is a personal-use file-transfer utility operated on the account owner's computer or server. It uses rclone and the Google Drive API to organize, transfer and verify files on the owner's instructions. This policy covers that utility and these public documentation pages; it does not describe a public audio-hosting service.

2. Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the utility may access:

  • Google account identity and storage-quota information, to check the intended account and available capacity.
  • File and folder names, identifiers, paths, sizes, timestamps, hashes and permissions, to identify and verify the intended destination.
  • File contents selected for transfer or integrity verification.

The requested Google Drive scope can permit reading, creating, changing and deleting files across the authorized account's Drive, excluding the Application Data folder. Actual operations are limited by the owner's instructions and the utility's selected-folder workflow. The authorization is not limited technically to files created by this utility.

3. Use and disclosure

Google user data is used to carry out the requested file-management operation and verify its result. File contents may be transferred between Google Drive and the operator-controlled computer or server. Operational records may include account identity, file metadata, checksums and verification results.

This utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. It does not automatically publish private files or change their sharing audience. A separate workflow that the owner explicitly authorizes to publish content is outside the scope of this transfer utility.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage, retention and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored privately on operator-controlled systems, not in this public site or its public repository. Files may be held locally while transfers and integrity checks are completed. Verification records may be retained to prevent duplicate or incorrect transfers and to document the results.

The owner controls retention and deletion of working files and Drive content. Revoking authorization does not automatically delete files that were already transferred or retained. No fixed retention period is promised by this personal-use utility.

5. Your control and revocation

The account owner can stop the utility, request deletion of retained working data, or revoke its Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access after existing access expires or is invalidated; it does not change the ownership of existing Drive files.

6. This public website

These pages are static documentation. They contain no application login form, analytics code, advertising, embedded media or Google Drive file listings, and set no application cookies. Visiting them does not authorize access to Google Drive.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process ordinary website-request information under its privacy statement. Google Drive file contents and OAuth credentials are not supplied to GitHub Pages by this utility.

7. Contact and updates

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the application's Google authorization screen. Do not post or send passwords, authorization codes, tokens or private files.

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
